# ORBITAL - scratchnotes

## Spaces

### Your Space (it's uncomfortably claustrophobic)

Trapped in a malfunctioned section of the station, you have but the remote console in the room with you, a limited supply of air canisters you can use to replenish the room, and a receptacle from which somebody outside could deposit more canisters.

## Real Space

Through the console you can manipulate Grabbie, an EVA robot free from your trapped area that can be used to navigate the physical world, collect supplies and deposit them back to your physical location.

Grabbie can fly in zero-G, and manipulate a limited amount of devices, switches and debris in the world. He can also approach emergency access points and jack them to enable your ability to connect to them as an Entry Vector in Cyber Mode

Your goal is to scavenge air supplies while working your way to the other side of the station, to replace a damaged transmitter through piloting Grabbie, so that a rescue can be raised.

### Cyberspace

From Cyberspace, you can fly through a digital representation of the station's machinery, spawning from any Entry Vector you've jacked to trigger doors, switches and other entities that will open more of the station for Grabbie to explore.

## Game Loop

- Remotely pilot two entities to navigate the station, one physical, and one digital.
- The physical entity can manipulate real objects, collect air canisters, and open new areas in Cyberspace.
- The digital entity can manipulate digital switches, opening new areas to explore in physical space.
- You need to keep yourself alive with air canisters, while piloting to the other side of the station to activate a beacon.
