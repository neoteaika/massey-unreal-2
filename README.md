#### Neo Te Aika - Massey Project Portfolio - 2025 Semester Two - Game Project - ORBITAL (August, 2025)
ORBITAL is a small adventure game as an operating service engineer on a damaged space station, awoken from cryo-stasis after the emergency system kicks in.
<img width="1122" height="524" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/58ee48ba-2fcb-456d-ba74-88f496b69b9a" />
Sealed in a room you cannot leave, you must utilize the interfaces available to you to save the station from freespin, and a six-hour collision course into Jupiter.
<img width="1122" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/648d4ee3-c61f-480f-ad8b-f8c32548251a" />
Two of your trusty devices are a remotely deployed maintenance robot that you can directly pilot and control to operate around the station, and a digital construct that allows you to explore the station's internal digital network, an entirely different perspective into the data and security systems of the station.
<img width="1122" height="583" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0dad09c3-04f7-4981-8595-e67f6390b5ce" />
Manage your internal air supply, fabricate new station components to complete repairs and correctly reallocate the station power systems on a quest to safety.
